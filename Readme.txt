# InterpoLoot
A Quality-of-Life mod for The Long Dark that replaces the standard "click and disappear" looting system with smooth interpolation animations. Items now seamlessly float from the world directly into your body/inventory!

## Features
- "QuickLoot" System: Bypass the vanilla popup inspection menu for a faster, smoother looting experience when picking up loose items in the world.
- Item Interpolation: Instead of items instantly teleporting into your inventory, they smoothly animate into you.
- Customizable:
  - Prefer the vanilla inspection UI for every click? Enable the `Always Use Vanilla Inspection Popup UI` in Mod Settings to keep the vanilla popup, but retain the new interpolation animation.
  - Disable the new consumption animation if you prefer the instant vanilla experience for eating and drinking from your inventory or radial menu.
  - By default, middle-mouse-button (MMB) lets you enter the vanilla inspect mode to rotate or harvest items without picking them up immediately. If you'd prefer another key, just set it!

## Requirements
This mod requires the following:
- [MelonLoader](https://melonwiki.xyz/#/?id=requirements) (v0.6.1 or later recommended)
- [ModSettings](https://github.com/zeobviouslyfakeacc/ModSettings) (v1.9 or later)

## Installation
1. Ensure you have MelonLoader installed in your *The Long Dark* directory.
2. Ensure you have the `ModSettings.dll` in your `Mods` folder.
3. Download the latest `InterpoLoot.dll` from the Releases page.
4. Drop `InterpoLoot.dll` into your `Mods` folder.
5. Launch the game. You can configure the mod in the `Options -> Mod Settings -> InterpoLoot` menu.

## Known Issues / Quirks
- Eating/Drinking from the inventory will close the inventory interface to show the consumption animation. This can be toggled off in Mod Settings.
